Welcome

A platform ticket lets you enter the railway platform to receive or see off a passenger. With this website you can book it in advance and skip the queue at the counter.

Book Platform Ticket

How It Works

  1. Check the station and fare on the Stations & Fares page.
  2. Fill in the booking form with your details.
  3. Submit the form and get your confirmation.
  4. Show the confirmation at the platform entrance.
